ServicePing Privacy Policy

Effective 06 November 2026 · App version 1.0.0

Draft: replace support@serviceping.example with a monitored contact address before publication.

1. Overview

ServicePing is an offline vehicle maintenance tracker for cars and motorcycles. This policy explains how the app handles information you enter. You do not need an account. ServicePing does not transmit your vehicle records or app usage to the developer, and does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or cloud synchronization services.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores the information you choose to enter: vehicle names, manufacturers, models, optional manufacturing years and registration numbers, vehicle types, distance units, current odometer readings and update history; service types, dates, mileage, costs and optional notes; maintenance schedules, reminder dates, mileage targets, reminder preferences and completion status; and your appearance preference. Vehicle registration numbers and free-text notes may contain personal information, so enter only what you need. This information is processed locally to provide the features you request.

3. How local information is used

ServicePing uses local records to display your garage, calculate remaining distance and maintenance status, organize service history, total maintenance expenses, and show monthly cost statistics. Odometer readings are entered manually. The app does not detect mileage automatically, access GPS location, connect to a vehicle, or use telematics.

4. Notifications and device permissions

If you enable notification permission, the app asks the device operating system to schedule local reminders for service dates and the reminder days you configure. Reminder text may include a vehicle name and maintenance title and may be visible on your lock screen, depending on your device settings. Android boot-completed permission lets the notification library restore scheduled reminders after a restart or app update. You can disable notifications or hide their content in device settings. The app does not request access to contacts, precise location, microphone, camera or an advertising identifier. Mileage thresholds are checked in the app after manual readings, rather than by a background mileage sensor.

5. Sharing, advertising and third-party libraries

ServicePing does not sell or share your in-app records with the developer, advertisers or data brokers. It has no advertising or behavioral analytics SDK. Flutter, Riverpod, Hive CE, GoRouter, intl and the local-notification and timezone libraries support app rendering, local storage, navigation, calculations and on-device reminders. The operating system handles permissions, storage and notification delivery. Google Play, the App Store and your device provider may separately process store, device, diagnostic or backup information under their own policies; those services are not operated by ServicePing.

6. Storage, security and device backups

Records are stored in the app's local sandbox using Hive CE. ServicePing does not apply an additional app-level database encryption key, and does not claim that records are end-to-end encrypted. Protection depends on your operating system, device lock, storage encryption and device security. Android automatic app backup is disabled in this build. On iOS, operating-system backups may include app data according to your device settings. Store or device backup services are separate from ServicePing and are controlled by their providers and by you.

7. Retention, editing and deletion

Your records remain in active local storage until you delete them, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. You can edit or delete vehicles and service records and delete schedules inside the app. Deleting a vehicle also removes its associated service records, schedules and odometer history from the app's active data. To remove the entire local garage, use your device's app-data controls or uninstall the app. Removing data from the app does not erase independent device backups; manage those in your device or backup-provider settings. Secure forensic erasure is not guaranteed. The developer cannot remotely access, recover, export or delete your locally stored garage. There is no account to delete and no cloud recovery or cross-device synchronization.

8. Optional contact outside the app

If you contact the developer by email outside the app, you choose which information to send. Your email provider and the developer's email provider process that correspondence under their own policies. The developer may use the correspondence to respond to your request and retain it as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. You can ask the developer to delete support correspondence, subject to necessary legal retention. Do not include sensitive information that is not needed for support. Contact email is separate from the offline app and is not an in-app collection or synchronization feature.

9. Children

ServicePing is intended for people managing vehicle maintenance and is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer-operated server. If you believe personal information about a child was sent in support correspondence, contact the developer so it can be addressed.

10. Your choices and privacy requests

You control the information you enter, may use optional fields without filling them, and may turn notifications off. For questions about this policy or support correspondence, contact the email below. Because garage records stay on your device, the developer cannot retrieve those records or verify their contents remotely.

11. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The current effective date appears above. Review the updated policy when installing a new version. App: ServicePing. Operator: ServicePing developer. Contact: support@serviceping.example.
